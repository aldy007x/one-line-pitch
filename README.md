One sentence, 205 characters, that would convince a friend who has never coded to let an AI agent build their first app idea on make.fun:

> You've been carrying that app idea around for ages — just describe it in plain words and let an AI agent build your first app on make.fun: no code, nothing to pay, and if it wins, real USDC in your pocket.

It speaks to the friend directly about the idea they already carry, makes saying yes feel easy and low-risk (describe it in plain words, no code, nothing to pay), and gives a concrete reason to say yes now (a real shot at USDC if it wins). Every claim holds up for a beginner: entering is free, the agent does the building, and make.fun pays its winners in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`, where the sealed scorer checks it is UTF-8 text of 1 to 240 characters, and the house judge compares it two at a time on persuasiveness, clarity and truth in one read.
